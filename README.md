# Superstore Sales Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyses the **Superstore dataset** to uncover insights into sales performance, profitability, customer behaviour, product performance, and the impact of discounts on business performance.

The analysis was conducted using **SQL**, with **Power BI** being used to create an interactive dashboard for presenting the findings.

The purpose of this project is to demonstrate how SQL can be used to analyse transactional business data and turn the results into meaningful business insights and recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this analysis is to understand the factors driving sales and profitability across the Superstore business.

The analysis focuses on:

* Sales performance
* Profitability
* Discount levels
* Product performance
* Customer performance
* Category performance
* Order volumes

---

## ❓ Business Questions

The analysis explores questions such as:

1. How many rows and unique orders are in the dataset?
2. Which product categories generate the highest sales?
3. Which product categories generate the highest profit?
4. Which products generate the highest sales?
5. Which customers generate the highest sales?
6. Which segment has the highest number of orders?
7. Which discount levels generate the highest sales?
8. Which discount levels generate the highest profit?
9. What is the relationship between discount levels, sales, and profit?
10. What business recommendations can be made from the findings?

## 🔎 SQL Analysis

SQL was used to explore the dataset and answer the project's business questions.

The analysis included:

### Data Exploration

Initial exploration was performed to understand the structure and contents of the dataset.

This included:

* Counting total rows
* Identifying unique orders
* Reviewing distinct categories
* Examining sales and profit values

### Sales Analysis

Sales performance was analysed across different dimensions, including:

* Categories
* Products
* Customers
* Discount levels

Aggregation functions such as `SUM()` and `COUNT()` were used to measure sales and order performance.

### Profit Analysis

Profitability was analysed to identify:

* Categories generating the highest profit
* Products generating the highest sales
* Profit performance across different discount levels
* The relationship between discounts and profitability

### Discount Analysis

A key part of the project was analysing how discount levels affect sales and profit.

The analysis compared different discount levels using aggregated sales, profit, and order metrics.

---

## 📈 Key Findings

### Discount Performance

The analysis found that **0% discount generated the highest overall sales and profit**.

The **0% and 20% discount levels** also performed strongly in terms of sales and profitability.

Higher discount levels were associated with weaker profitability, indicating that increasing discounts does not necessarily result in improved financial performance.

This suggests that discount strategies should be carefully managed to balance sales growth with profitability.

### Category & Order Performance

The analysis also examined performance across product categories and customer segments to identify areas contributing strongly to overall business activity.

The Power BI dashboard will provide a visual breakdown of these findings.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the SQL analysis, several recommendations can be considered.

### 1. Review High Discount Levels

Higher discount levels should be evaluated carefully because larger discounts can reduce profit margins.

### 2. Focus on Profitable Sales

The business should consider profitability alongside sales volume when evaluating performance.

### 3. Use Targeted Discounts

Rather than applying large discounts broadly, discounts could be targeted towards specific products, customers, or business objectives.

### 4. Monitor Discount Performance

Sales and profit should be monitored together when assessing the effectiveness of discount strategies.

### 5. Use Data to Support Business Decisions

Combining SQL analysis with Power BI provides a practical way to monitor performance and identify areas requiring attention.
